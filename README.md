## 📖 About the Project
**BackOffice AI** is an AI-powered, mobile-first smart business management platform built for **one-person businesses**, solo founders, tutors, freelancers, salons, retail shops, and small clinics. 

Instead of juggling separate spreadsheets, invoicing tools, customer notebooks, and accounting apps, BackOffice AI brings intelligent administration, customer and payment tracking, live cash-flow forecasting, automated reports, and an integrated AI Business Assistant together into a single, unified workspace.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **📊 Real-Time Financial Dashboard:** Instantly track Total Income, Total Expenses, and Net Cash Position in Indian Rupees (`₹`), with custom business banner and logo personalization.
* **👥 Customer & Business Management:** Organize client records, business profiles, and localized Indian address/currency settings.
* **🧾 Invoices, Payments & WhatsApp Reminders:** Generate and track invoices, monitor pending dues, record banking/payment history, and send WhatsApp reminders.
* **📈 Income, Expenses & Cash-Flow Forecasting:** Visualize income vs. expense trends with 30–90 day cash-flow forecasts and what-if business scenarios.
* **🤖 Integrated AI Business Assistant:** An intelligent assistant that analyzes your real business data to answer operational questions and guide daily decisions.
* **📑 Automated Reports & Excel Export:** Export clean accounting and transaction data anytime for tax filing or sharing.
* **🔐 Secure Owner & Super Admin Panel:** Dedicated administrator credentials and workspace privacy to lock down sensitive financial and customer data.

## 🛠️ How to Create an Account & Get Started

Follow these quick steps on the [Live Application](https://hemanth9760.github.io/Backoffice-ai-app/) to set up your workspace:

1. **Create Business Account:**
   * Open [https://hemanth9760.github.io/Backoffice-ai-app/](https://hemanth9760.github.io/Backoffice-ai-app/) and click **Create Business Account**.
   * Enter your **Owner's Full Name**, **Username**, **Email Address**, **10-digit Mobile Number**, and **Password** (minimum 6 characters).
   * Click **Create Account & Continue**.

2. **Select Your Business Category:**
   * Choose the category that best fits your business to personalize your workspace:
     * *Education* (Tutors & tuition centres)
     * *Freelancing* (Designers & developers)
     * *Salon* (Beauty & hair services)
     * *Retail* (Shops & stores)
     * *Healthcare* (Small clinics)
     * *Other* (Other businesses)
   * Click **Continue**.

3. **Enter Business Details:**
   * Fill in your **Business Name**, **Description**, **Owner Name**, **Address** (Street, City, PIN Code, State, Country), **Business Phone/Email**, and **Opening Cash / Bank Balance (`₹`)**.
   * Click **Continue**.

4. **Create Your Owner Admin Panel:**
   * Set up your private **Admin Username**, **Admin Email**, **Admin Password**, and optional recovery mobile number to become the Super Admin of your workspace.
   * Click **Create Admin Panel**.

5. **Launch Your Workspace:**
   * **Start with an empty workspace:** Begin with a clean slate and add your own real business records.
   * **Explore with demonstration records:** Launch immediately with pre-loaded sample data (such as a Home Tutor business demo) to test all features right away.
